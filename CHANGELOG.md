## 1.1.0

- Added smooth interactive rotational dragging gesture (`onPanUpdate`) to rotate the wheel continuously with touch gestures.
- Added Harmonic Progressions feature (`HarmonicProgression`) to highlight and number popular chord progressions (Worship/Pop $I - V - vi - IV$, Andalusian Cadence $i - VII - VI - V$, Jazz $ii - V - I$).
- Added step sequence badges (1, 2, 3, 4) overlay on progression chords.
- Enhanced theme adaptability and touch gesture precision.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release of `flutter_circle_of_fifths`.
- Interactive 12-sector Circle of Fifths wheel widget with custom `CustomPainter`.
- Full support for Major and Relative Minor modes with 12:00 rotation.
- Diatonic scale breakdown card showing scale notes, degree numbers (1-8), and halftone interval formulas.
- Material 3 theme adaptive (Light & Dark Mode).
- Custom `CircleOfFifthsTheme` for customizing center branding label and sector colors.
- Ready-to-use `CircleOfFifthsSheet` Draggable Scrollable Modal BottomSheet with SegmentedButton view switcher.
